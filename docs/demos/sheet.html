About Flint

Flint is a flat Material 2 library for building web apps with plain HTML — one stylesheet, one small script, no build step and no dependencies.

Surfaces are 1px-bordered cards, never shadows. State layers are flat fills at a shared duration. The density is 44px controls, not the 56px Material default.

The overlay layer is the native <dialog>: focus trap, inert background, Escape and top-layer stacking come from the platform, so flint.js stays small.

Bottom sheets share that layer — same scrim token, same focus protocol, same scroll lock, one rule of html:has(dialog[open]).

The drag handle is a glyph, not a gesture: sheets close on scrim tap, Escape or their own actions. Drag-to-dismiss is not supported and not claimed.

Every component degrades honestly: without JavaScript the page renders and works; overlays simply stay closed until you open them.

Long content scrolls inside the body while the handle and title stay pinned — flexbox, not JavaScript.

Fields, buttons, lists and selection controls all compose inside sheets exactly as they do on a page.

Reduced-motion users get instant open and close; the transitions simply don't run.

Everything above is a documented decision, not an accident — the docs say what deviates from Material 2 and why.
