What's new in 0.4

Dialogs and bottom sheets land on a shared overlay layer built on the native <dialog> element.

The focus trap, the inert background, Escape-to-close and top-layer stacking are platform work — the library ships none of them.

The scrim is one token, --fl-scrim, shared by every overlay type including the drawer.

Open and close are both animated in CSS: entry via @starting-style, exit via allow-discrete on display and overlay.

Scroll lock is one rule: html:has(dialog[open]). No body-class bookkeeping in flint.js.

Long dialog content scrolls in the body while the title and action row stay pinned — flexbox, not JavaScript.

Destructive confirmations get a dedicated danger text button in --fl-error.

Forms work inside dialogs: the field family composes unchanged, and Enter submits through method="dialog".

Everything degrades honestly: without JavaScript, dialogs stay hidden and the rest of the page keeps working.

Reduced-motion users get instant open and close — the transitions simply don't run.
